Application Process: Steps to Get a Garage Building Permit in Calgary 
1. Determine the Type of Garage 
· Detached or Attached: Detached garages are more common and have different 
requirements than attached ones. 
· Size and Use: If you plan to include electrical, plumbing, or heating, additional permits 
may be required. 
2. Check Zoning and Bylaws 
· Use the City of Calgary's myProperty tool to check your zoning. 
· Ensure your garage complies with setback, height, and lot coverage rules. 
3. Prepare Your Application 
· You’ll need: 
· A site plan showing the location of the garage on your property. 
· Construction drawings (floor plan, elevations, cross-sections). 
· Owner authorization if you're not the property owner. 
4. Apply for the Permit 
· Submit your application online through the City of Calgary’s ePermit system. 
· You may also apply in person at the Planning Services Centre. 
5. Pay the Fees 
· Fees vary based on the size and scope of the project. Expect to pay around $200–$550. 
6. Wait for Approval 
· Review times vary, but simple garage permits are often processed within a few weeks. 
7. Schedule Inspections 
· Once construction begins, you’ll need to book inspections (e.g., foundation, framing,

Additional Permits 
If your garage includes: 
· Electrical work → Apply for an electrical permit. 
· Heating or plumbing → Separate permits are required.
